# Agua en Seguimiento - Customer Personas
Version: 1.0 | Week 12

## Research status

These are fictional, provisional personas informed by the
previous briefs and synthetic testing. They are not interviewed
customers. Ages, routines and quotes are design hypotheses.
Update these profiles when real-user research corrects them.

## Persona 1 - Laura, the coordinator

- Name: Laura.
- Age: 54.
- Context: Coordinates a small program supporting households
  with water leaks. Uses WhatsApp and basic applications,
  reads slowly and has limited time to review cases.
- Need in her words (fictional): "I need to know which cases
  need attention, who is responsible and what happens next."
- What she does today: We hypothesize that she combines
  WhatsApp, calls and a spreadsheet. This is unconfirmed.
- What she would pay: Unknown. Her organization is a possible
  customer, but its budget and willingness to pay need testing.
- What would make her quit: Repeated data entry, confusing
  statuses, too many required fields or no advantage over Excel.
- Main task: Find a pending case, record its next action and
  keep a repair pending until verification is documented.
- Testing behavior: May interpret green as "resolved."
  Hesitates when labels are unclear and may quit silently.

## Persona 2 - Rosa, the household representative

- Name: Rosa.
- Age: 46.
- Context: Fictional adult representing a household with a
  reported water leak. Needs clear information about support
  and must coordinate access around daily responsibilities.
- Need in her words (fictional): "Tell me what help is available,
  when someone will come and what to do if the leak continues."
- What she does today: We hypothesize that she contacts a
  plumber or support program by phone or WhatsApp and follows
  up herself. This is unconfirmed.
- What she would pay: Unknown. The proposed pilot is free
  for households; external funding is not yet secured.
- What would make her quit: Unexpected charges, missed visits
  without explanation, unnecessary personal-data requests or
  being told the case is resolved while water loss continues.
- Main task: Report whether the repair worked and understand
  the next step if it did not.
- Access: Does not need an app account in the initial build.
  The coordinator records her confirmation through an agreed
  contact channel, with permission.

## Validation priorities

1. Interview a real coordinator about their current workflow.
2. Document a recent household experience with leak follow-up.
3. Check which information each person actually needs.
4. Test comprehension of "repair reported," "verification
   pending" and "verified resolved."
5. Investigate organizational willingness to pay.
